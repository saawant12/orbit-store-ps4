# Save your Browse and Add-ons filters

**For Orbit Store 0.2.0 for PS4.**

Choose your filters, search and sort order in **Browse**, then open **Saved filters**. Enter a name and choose **Save filters**. Select a saved entry later to apply it again in one go. Saved filters include your **Genre** choices.

![Saved filters in the PS4 TV app's Browse, with example presets](../assets/0.0.1/native-saved-filters.jpg)

*Saved filters in the PS4 TV app, with example Browse presets.*

Browse and Add-ons have separate lists. Saving under an existing name replaces that entry; **Delete** removes only the saved selection, not any games or downloads. Orbit keeps up to 50 saved entries on the PS4.

The TV app, the browser version and a paired [Orbit Zero](orbit-zero.md) share the PS4’s list in all four interfaces. Orbit Zero needs the PS4 connected to read or change the list; browsing on the computer still works while it’s disconnected. A filter can return different results as the catalogue changes. Opening a game from filtered Browse results selects a download option that matches those filters. Opening it from Discover or Favourites doesn’t apply a saved Browse filter to its download choices.
